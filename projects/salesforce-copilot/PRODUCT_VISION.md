# Salesforce Copilot

## Vision

Salesforce Copilot is an AI-powered assistant that helps Salesforce professionals understand, troubleshoot, improve, document, and learn Salesforce faster.

Instead of searching Setup, reading documentation, or reverse-engineering Flows, users interact with an intelligent copilot that provides explanations, recommendations, documentation, and guidance in seconds.

Salesforce Copilot acts like a Senior Salesforce Consultant available 24 hours a day.

---

## Mission

Reduce the time required to understand and manage Salesforce organizations while helping administrators become more productive and confident.

---

## Long-Term Goal

Become the everyday AI companion for Salesforce professionals.
